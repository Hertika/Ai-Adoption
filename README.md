# 🤖 AI Adoption & Workforce Impact Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Artificial Intelligence is rapidly becoming part of the modern workplace, but how widely are organizations actually adopting AI?

This project analyzes **80,000 employee records** to understand how AI is being adopted across different departments, locations, experience levels, and AI tools — and how this adoption relates to **productivity, ROI, hours saved, and employee satisfaction**.

The goal was to move beyond simply measuring AI usage and understand **how AI adoption is shaping workforce performance.**

---

## 🎯 Business Questions

This analysis focuses on answering:

- Which AI tools are most widely adopted?
- Which departments are adopting AI the most?
- How does AI adoption vary across locations?
- Does AI adoption relate to higher productivity?
- Which AI tools generate better ROI?
- How many working hours are being saved through AI?
- Does AI usage influence employee satisfaction?
- Does experience level or training affect AI adoption?

---

## 📊 Dataset

- **Total Records:** 80,000 employees
- **AI Tools:** ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity
- **Departments:** Finance, HR, IT, Legal, Marketing, Operations, Sales, Support
- **Locations:** Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Chennai, Delhi, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Pune
- **Experience Levels:** Fresher, Junior, Mid, Senior

### Key Metrics

- AI Usage Hours
- Tasks Completed
- Productivity Score
- Hours Saved per Week
- Monthly Cost Savings
- ROI %
- Satisfaction Score
- Training Completed

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Differences in AI adoption across business functions.
- Variation in productivity and ROI between AI tools.

- Differences in employee satisfaction across AI tools.

This project demonstrates how employee-level data can be transformed into **business insights around AI adoption, workforce productivity, and organizational performance**.
